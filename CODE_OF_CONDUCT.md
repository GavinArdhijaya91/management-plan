# Code of Conduct

## Our promise

We want Siapin to be a welcoming place for everyone — regardless of background, experience level, language, or where you come from.

Whether you are a first-time contributor fixing a typo, a designer suggesting better words, or an engineer opening a big pull request — you belong here.

## How we treat each other

**Please:**

- Be kind and respectful. Assume good intentions first.
- Use clear, simple language. Not everyone is a native English speaker.
- Welcome newcomers and help them learn.
- Give and receive feedback gracefully. Focus on the idea, not the person.
- Respect different opinions. Disagree without being unkind.

**Please do not:**

- Use harassing, discriminatory, or insulting language
- Make personal attacks or unwanted advances
- Share someone's private information without permission
- Spam, troll, or deliberately disrupt conversations
- Harass anyone for any reason — online or offline

This applies everywhere in the project: issues, pull requests, discussions, commits, and any community space.

## If something goes wrong

If you see or experience behavior that does not follow this code:

1. If you feel safe, you can kindly let the person know.
2. Otherwise, contact the maintainers privately through **GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting** (for sensitive matters) or by opening a minimal issue titled “CoC concern — please contact me privately”.

We will:

- Listen and take it seriously
- Keep it as private as possible
- Look at what happened fairly, considering context
- Decide on a response that fits — from a private conversation to a temporary or permanent ban, depending on the situation

We will not share your identity without your permission.

## Our standards

This code is based on the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The key idea is simple: **help each person feel safe to contribute**.

## Thank you

Siapin exists to help small businesses work better together. That starts with us — the people building it — working better together too.

Thank you for being part of it. 🌿
